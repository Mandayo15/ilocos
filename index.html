About Ilocos

Ilocos, located in the northwestern region of the Philippines, is a province known for its stunning beaches, rich cultural heritage, and vibrant traditions. Divided into Ilocos Norte and Ilocos Sur, it offers a mix of adventure, relaxation, and history.

From the UNESCO World Heritage city of Vigan to the serene shores of Pagudpud, Ilocos invites travelers to experience its charm, taste its delicacies, and embrace its warmth.

Top Attractions

Vigan City

Step into a colonial past through Vigan’s cobblestone streets and ancestral homes.

Pagudpud Beach

Relax on the powdery white sands of Pagudpud, the "Boracay of the North."

Kapurpurawan Rock Formation

Marvel at Ilocos’ natural limestone masterpiece sculpted by the wind and sea.

Sand Dunes

Experience thrilling 4x4 rides across the iconic La Paz and Paoay Sand Dunes.
